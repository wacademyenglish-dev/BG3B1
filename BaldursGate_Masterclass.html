The Digital Revolution

What you just experienced is a tiny fraction of what Baldur's Gate 3 achieved. For decades, video games struggled to capture the true freedom of a Tabletop RPG.

BG3 didn't just digitize combat; it digitized consequences. By integrating the D20 dice system directly into the dialogue and environment, it forced millions of players to live with their choices, just like we did today with English Grammar.
